# WormholeLab methodology

## Claim discipline

WormholeLab separates mathematical admissibility, physical existence, stability, traversability, model fit, and observational identification. A successful numerical demonstration at one level does not automatically establish a stronger level.

## Browser-scale approximations

Modules 3–6 deliberately use reduced-order transparent models where a full calculation would require strong-field ray tracing, radiative transfer, metric-specific perturbation theory, or numerical relativity. The UI labels these approximations. Their purpose is to expose the observable-to-inference chain without pretending to solve a more difficult problem than they do.

## Proposed inference constructs

### SSP
`Dop` is derived from a declared admissible route set and cost functional. It is not a new invariant or replacement for relativistic interval.

### WIF
Model family, parameters, priors, forward model/likelihood, rival, and held-out prediction should be declared before escalation.

### OSCT
`H0` contains one accessible source/propagation region. `H1` adds a declared second causal channel. The Bayes factor is conditional on those two generative models and their priors.

### MMWT
Geometry parameters are shared across channels; source/instrument nuisance parameters can be channel-specific. Correlated calibration and theory errors must not be ignored in research applications.

## Evidence ladder

0. anomaly in one processed product
1. survives calibration/systematics
2. specified alternative outperforms strengthened rivals
3. one shared geometry explains appropriately independent channels
4. predeclared quantitative prediction succeeds
5. independent replication with different pipelines/instruments
